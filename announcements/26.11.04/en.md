# Update Notes
Please finish your current run before updating, as updating may affect runs in progress.
# Default Branch Update
---
### Items and Balance
- Items that previously spawned Purple Wisps now spawn Blue Wisps instead.
- Black Eyebrow Pouch now remembers the colors of stored Wisps, so it can release them in their original colors even after you resume a run.
- Fixed Magical Conch sometimes removing unrelated Wisps when it restored eggs.
- Fixed an issue where Fading Laws prevented damage but failed to trigger effects that normally activate when characters such as Nik take damage.
### Controller and Combat
- With “Attack with Right Stick” turned off, you can still use the right stick to select targets without firing. If you turn off “Controller Aim Pointer,” it now stays off when you start a new run.
- Fixed the first shot sometimes firing in the wrong direction after selecting a target. Holding the right stick now lets you take manual control of your attack direction again.
- Fixed aim assist sometimes failing to select enemies behind walls even when a wall-piercing or splitting shot could reach them.
- Fixed automatic attacks interrupting grenade aiming and throwing.
### Co-op and Levels
- Fixed players sometimes being unable to control their character after a failed attempt to join co-op from the bar.
- Fixed a stale connection after a full lobby rejected a player, which could leave the group waiting to start. If someone leaves and everyone remaining is ready, the entry countdown now restarts.
- Fixed hosts being able to lower the player limit in a way that hid an occupied slot.
- Fixed teammates sometimes being unable to enter a boss room after a player left the gathering point while performing another action.
- Fixed the basketball sometimes getting stuck in midair and becoming impossible to pick up after a failed grab in co-op. Teammates’ Hatchmon eggs now also grey out and return to color correctly as combat starts and ends.
- Fixed the God of AI being able to take damage before the fight started, which could leave the room doors stuck.
- Fixed missing platforms in the God of AI room and platforms in some boss rooms that players could fall through.
- Fixed regular bosses being counted toward elite boss progression and causing problems with the next target.
### Interface and Saves
- Fixed some shop stands appearing out of place or showing mirrored prices, and removed stray horizontal trails from character previews on the results screen.
- Restored Veteran Coin’s Codex entry and corrected some Blackdog Patch icons and localized text.
- Improved save performance by reducing repeated saves at the end of a successful solo run.
