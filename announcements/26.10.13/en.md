# October 13 Update Notes
Please finish your current run before updating, as updating may cause issues with in-progress runs.
# Default Branch Update
---
### Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where lightning strikes from Skyrend and standard projectiles from Jedon failed to trigger Popcorn correctly.
- Reduced instances of Origin projectiles damaging the player when combined with Popcorn.
- Fixed an issue where melee attack intervals could increase at high attack speeds.
- Fixed Glitz's projectile-count affixes and their upgrades not taking effect correctly, and corrected their co-op synchronization.
- Fixed badge generation with Glass Dice. New badges now use the affix pool for the player’s current weapon, falling back to the general pool if no eligible options are available.
- Fixed an issue where holding the fire button allowed shooting during reloads.
- Fixed targeting configurations for certain egg-type enemies and the second phase of the God of Screens fight.
- Fixed an issue where the host could not pick up basketballs thrown by other players in co-op, and physics stopped working on some liftable objects after they were left idle.
- Fixed issues when multiple players used a faith machine at the same time in co-op. Failed attempts to use a dice machine no longer consume coins, keys, or free unlock uses.
- Fixed an interaction error with the dance room machine that could disrupt subsequent pickups.
- Fixed an issue where question marks spawned by butterflies in the Temple of Psychedelism failed to deal damage correctly in co-op.
- Fixed an issue where some enemies could unexpectedly regain health after taking damage in co-op.
- Fixed weapons not being locked correctly in basketball rooms generated by Hexjars.
- Fixed issues when entering certain boss rooms and improved stability during floor transitions.
- Fixed old encounters still triggering completion logic after returning to the bar on death or restarting a run.
- Fixed the map close button extending beyond the screen on narrow displays, and unwanted camera movement after closing the map.
- Fixed icon display in the dress-up and ban panels, duplicate Hatchmon counts on the stats screen, and emote previews remaining visible after closing the emote screen.
- Added the missing localized confirmation button to the Storm Breach level selection screen.
- Fixed players getting stuck on terrain, incorrect doors, and background and masking issues in certain rooms. Corrected some hairstyle configurations.
- Corrected the reward quantity for the Dance Party task.
### Improvements
- Reduced the hold time for dismantling weapons and items from 2 seconds to 0.8 seconds.
- Shortened item pickup banner display times.
- Improved simultaneous item pickup handling in co-op and reduced interference from unnecessary targets during automatic attacks.
- Removed unnecessary network speed checks and the associated wait when entering the bar.
- Removed the overhead icon for Maneuver Gear and the F4 survey feedback shortcut.
