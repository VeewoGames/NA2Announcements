# October 22 Update Notes
Please finish your current run before updating, as updating may cause issues with in-progress runs.
# Default Branch Update
---
### Adjustments and Improvements
- Co-op room lists now show rooms hosted on different client versions.
- Improved how controller targeting works with automatic attacks, including attack range checks and automatic attack timing for certain special weapons.
- Added missing spawn protection for Aphrodite, the God of Emojis, and the God of Pachinko. Spawn protection for the God of Emojis and the God of Pachinko is now 0.5 seconds.
- Adjusted the font on the results screen.
- Updated some localized text.
### Combat and Item Fixes
- Fixed Wisps affected by Finch’s Diary failing to open locked chests and incorrect Wisp consumption when players opened a chest simultaneously in co-op. Reduced the processing cost of chest detection.
- Fixed weapon level and affix data from one drop affecting subsequent drops.
- Fixed existing affixes failing to upgrade when no new affixes were available to roll.
- Fixed the level and affixes rolled for a new weapon being lost when rerolling weapons with dice.
- Fixed Sakurafall’s level synchronization after downgrading, experience consumption and reward progress while its level was locked, and experience jar calculations.
- Fixed incomplete restoration of Sakurafall’s bound effects and incorrect affix synchronization after reconnecting. Removed bindings no longer become active again on reconnect.
- Fixed Warming’s standard projectiles not receiving weapon projectile-count bonuses correctly.
- Fixed Ascend allowing swords to be stored repeatedly at no cost after changing rooms.
- Fixed issues with Voltedge’s dash penetration at low frame rates, and invincibility remaining active after an interrupted dash.
- Fixed Verdict’s light spear effect failing to work after delayed loading.
- Fixed issues with subsequent charging for Champion.
- Fixed eggs protected by Aged Acorn bypassing the egg display limit.
- Fixed an issue involving Cosmic Bag, red hearts, and rainbow hearts.
- Fixed Gem Jar and Mirror Jar becoming transparent when their effects were triggered again by Gramophone.
- Fixed Great Wonder Block spawning groups of enemies in minigame rooms where weapons are disabled.
- Fixed enabling unlimited Patch energy not immediately refilling it.
### Boss, Room, and Co-op Fixes
- Fixed some characters being unable to move after landing at the start of a run.
- Fixed the exit failing to appear after quickly defeating Aphrodite, leaving players unable to leave the boss room.
- Fixed the fight failing to end correctly when the God of Emojis’ split forms were defeated rapidly at high attack speeds.
- Fixed certain hiding or teleporting enemies becoming stuck in an invalid state, preventing room completion and doors from opening.
- Fixed incorrect damage protection calculations for certain bosses and corrected the relevant protection settings for final bosses across difficulty levels.
- Fixed a potential crash when leaving boss rooms.
- Fixed players potentially becoming stuck when a Faith Room closed unexpectedly.
- Fixed an issue where a new save could incorrectly enter the Blackdog floor on floor 5 after a co-op client disconnected.
- Fixed failing to enter the results screen after dying and reconnecting in co-op.
- Fixed gathering portals not updating their waiting state after a co-op player disconnected or left.
- Fixed dance QTEs still triggering after players left the dance room, incorrect teleports, and lingering countdown displays.
- Fixed stale held-object states after changing floors in co-op preventing skills and throwing from working.
- Fixed random characters not loading completely from other players’ perspectives in multiplayer.
- Fixed rewards being claimable repeatedly in the Artisan Temple.
- Fixed pickups near certain exits, teleport points, and NPCs not moving aside promptly, interfering with interaction.
### Save, Task, and Control Fixes
- Fixed random Patches not being randomized again after a quick restart.
- Fixed co-op victories not counting correctly toward clear progress in Fate tasks.
- Fixed the main menu not refreshing after downloading a cloud save.
- Fixed some story dialogue appearing incomplete or being cut off.
- Fixed PlayStation and Xbox controller recognition issues and incorrect QTE button icons.
- Fixed vibration settings not taking effect correctly after vibration was disabled or its intensity was changed.
- Fixed an issue where teleporting or interrupting an interaction could leave a hold-to-interact action active and interfere with subsequent controls.
- Fixed interaction prompts appearing above the fade overlay while it was displayed.
- Corrected the Abyssal Difficulty unlock prompt to clarify that it is unlocked through a task.
